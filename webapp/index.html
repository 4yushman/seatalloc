Try this in the demo

  1. Set capacity 3, press Apply.
  2. Register 5 people — the first 3 get seats, the rest queue.
  3. A MEMBER jumps ahead of a GENERAL registration.
  4. Cancel a confirmed seat → the next in the queue gets an offer.
  5. Accept it (or let the TTL lapse and press Expire to recycle the seat).
  6. Download the manifest CSV and replay the audit log.
